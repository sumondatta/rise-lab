Oklahoma State University · Fall 2023, 2024, 2025 · Undergraduate & graduate

Oklahoma State University · Spring 2025 · Graduate
I developed this course to give graduate students experience with soil moisture and canopy temperature sensors, soil water balance models, and crop models for irrigation management.
