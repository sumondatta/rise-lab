Interested in Joining the Group?

Study irrigation water management with us

I'm interested in hearing from prospective students, especially those planning to pursue a Ph.D. Applicants should have a B.S. or M.S. in Agricultural Engineering, Biosystems Engineering, or Computer Science, either completed or with only the thesis remaining. Computer Science applicants should plan to take the FE exam before applying.

If you meet these requirements, send me your CV and a cover letter describing your research interests and how your experience fits our work. I will contact shortlisted applicants.
