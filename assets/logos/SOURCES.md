# Funder logo sources

The logos are the original artwork supplied by the organizations or their
academic and program partners. Their colors and proportions are preserved.
They identify the organizations supporting the work listed on this website.

Some units use a parent identity: OSU for Rural Renewal, CEAT, and Ag Research;
Texas Tech for the Davis College Water Center; the University of Maine for its
Water Resources Research Institute; and USDA ARS for the Ogallala Aquifer Program.
The Ogallala card is labeled with its parent agency. No separate program logo is implied.
